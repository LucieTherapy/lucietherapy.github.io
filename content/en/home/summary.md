### CBT and ACT therapy in English and French, in Brighton & Hove and online {: .section-subtitle }

I am a **chartered psychologist** offering short and long-term support for adolescents (16+) and adults — **in person in Hove and Brighton**, East Sussex, and **online** for French speakers and expatriates wherever they live.

My practice is grounded in **evidence-based approaches**: **Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT)** and **Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT)**, alongside mindfulness and skills drawn from Dialectical Behaviour Therapy.

As a French clinical psychologist, I lived for several years in the United States and Canada, and practised in Singapore, before settling in the UK. I draw on that experience to offer compassionate, personalised support that respects your identity and your life journey.

I also provide [**CBT supervision**](/cbt-supervision/) for therapists and trainees, as an AFTCC-certified supervisor.

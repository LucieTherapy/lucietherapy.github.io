I offer **online therapy sessions** by secure video link, in **English and in French**, for adults and adolescents from the age of 16. I work with clients across the **UK, Europe and Asia**, with sessions scheduled around your time zone.

### Who online therapy suits

Online sessions remove the constraint of geography. They are often the right choice if you:

- Live outside the Brighton and Hove area, or elsewhere in the UK
- Are a **French speaker living abroad** and would rather work in your first language
- Travel frequently for work, or have recently relocated
- Find travelling to appointments difficult because of health, caring responsibilities or a demanding schedule
- Want to continue therapy through a move, a long trip or a period abroad
- Are looking for a practitioner trained in a specific approach that is not available locally

If you would prefer to meet in person, I also see clients at a practice in Hove — see [therapy in Brighton and Hove](/therapist-brighton-hove/).

### How I work online

I adapt all my therapeutic tools to the online format rather than simply talking over video. Shared whiteboards and screen-based exercises make it possible to do the practical work of **CBT** and **ACT** as effectively as in the room.

The same standards of **ethics, confidentiality and quality of care** apply as for in-person sessions. With your consent, I can also coordinate with other professionals involved in your care — your GP, psychiatrist or dietitian.

Online work is not the right format for everything. For some severe or complex difficulties, in-person support allows for a more thorough assessment, and I will say so if I think that is the case. I have written a candid account of [the benefits and limits of online therapy](/blog/benefits-of-online-therapy/).

### What I can help with

Anxiety and panic, depression and low mood, burnout, grief, relationship difficulties, low self-esteem, perfectionism, sleep difficulties, identity questions, and the particular strains of **living between countries** — isolation, cultural adjustment, supporting ageing parents from a distance, and helping children settle into a new school system. My article on [life abroad](/blog/therapy-for-expats-living-abroad/) goes into this in more depth.

### Background

I am a **chartered psychologist** (BPS, CPsychol 716681), trained as a clinical psychologist in France with a Master's degree from the Université Paris Cité, three years of specialised **CBT training at the AFTCC**, and further training in **ACT** with Steven C. Hayes and Russ Harris. I have more than **twelve years of clinical experience** across France, Singapore and the UK, and I have lived in the United States, Canada and Singapore myself.

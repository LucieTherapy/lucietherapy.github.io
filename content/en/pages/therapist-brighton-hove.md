I am a **chartered psychologist** (BPS, CPsychol 716681) practising **Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT)** and **Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT)** in **Brighton and Hove**. I see adults and adolescents from the age of 16, in **English and in French**.

### Where I practise

I see clients in person at **Brighton and Hove Psychotherapy**, 49 Church Road, Hove, BN3 2BE — a few minutes from Hove station and on the main bus routes into central Brighton. The practice serves clients from across **Brighton, Hove, and the wider East Sussex** area, including Portslade, Shoreham, Lewes and Worthing.

In-person appointments are available on **Mondays, Wednesdays and Fridays**. If those times do not work, or if travelling to Hove is difficult, I also offer [online sessions](/french-speaking-online-therapy/).

### What I can help with

I work with people facing a wide range of difficulties, including:

- Anxiety, worry, panic and social anxiety
- Depression and low mood
- Burnout and emotional exhaustion
- Stress and difficulty coping with pressure
- Low self-esteem and self-criticism
- Grief and bereavement
- Relationship and interpersonal difficulties
- Sleep difficulties
- Perfectionism and performance anxiety
- Difficult life transitions, including relocation and adjusting to life in the UK
- Identity questions and self-understanding

### How I work

My practice is grounded in **evidence-based therapy**. CBT is a structured, collaborative and goal-oriented approach: together we identify the patterns of thinking, feeling and behaving that keep a difficulty going, and work on practical change in everyday life. ACT adds a focus on **psychological flexibility** — learning to make room for difficult thoughts and feelings while taking action guided by what genuinely matters to you.

I also draw on **mindfulness** practices and clinical skills from **Dialectical Behaviour Therapy (DBT)** where these are likely to be useful.

You can read more about each approach in my articles on [CBT](/blog/what-is-cbt-cognitive-behavioural-therapy/), [ACT](/blog/acceptance-and-commitment-therapy-act/) and [mindfulness](/blog/mindfulness-in-therapy/).

### Background

I trained as a **clinical psychologist in France**, with a Bachelor's and Master's degree in Clinical Psychology and Psychopathology from the Université Paris Cité, followed by three years of specialised CBT training at the **AFTCC** (French Association for Behavioural and Cognitive Therapy) and a two-year advanced programme qualifying me as a **CBT supervisor**.

I have over **twelve years of clinical experience** across the public and private health sectors in France, Singapore and the UK — from psychiatric assessment in hospital settings to private practice. Having lived in the United States, Canada and Singapore before settling in Brighton, I am particularly used to working with people from **multicultural backgrounds** and with those adjusting to life in a new country.

Individual sessions are **£90 for 50 minutes**. If you are considering therapy, the first step is simply to get in touch — you are welcome to ask questions before committing to anything, and there is no expectation that you arrive with everything already worked out.
